# A Greenhouse for the Community Garden: Funding Request

Source: [inputs.md](inputs.md).

## The Need

The community garden's current tool shed has a confirmed leaking roof, identified in a site inspection this March, which has already damaged stored seed trays. Separately from that repair need, real demand exists for a proper growing space: 40 people signed an interest sheet across the group's last two community meetings specifically wanting access to a greenhouse.

## What This Would Actually Change

A greenhouse would give the group a dedicated, weatherproof growing space distinct from the shed, addressing the demand shown by the 40 sign-ups, not the shed's separate leak, which would still need its own repair.

Three volunteers have already committed in writing to weekly greenhouse upkeep, which is a stronger indicator of sustained use than the sign-up interest alone: it shows people willing to commit ongoing time, not just attend a meeting.

## Cost

Two quotes for the greenhouse structure have been requested and have not yet come back. This request is submitted now to begin the committee's own process in parallel, with the confirmed figure to follow as soon as it is available. No estimate is given here in place of a confirmed number.

## Limitations

This request covers the greenhouse only. It does not include the shed roof repair, which is a separate, already-identified need the group is pursuing through a different route.

## Next Steps

This application starts the committee's review process. Once the two quotes are back, the group will submit the confirmed figure to complete the funding request; committee approval at this stage does not itself commit funds until that figure is confirmed.
