# Human Review Checklist

Check these before the case goes to the decision-maker.

- [ ] You'd stand behind every figure, and no made-up estimate stands in for an unknown
- [ ] Every example names a specific fact from the real evidence, not wording that would fit any case
- [ ] The case is written for someone who wasn't there, not for an insider
- [ ] The risk or limitations section is accurate, and nobody removed or softened it to look stronger
- [ ] Nothing suggests the document alone finishes the process or guarantees an outcome
