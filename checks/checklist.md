# Human Review Checklist

Run through this before the case goes to the decision-maker.

- [ ] Every figure is one you are actually prepared to stand behind, with no invented estimate standing in for an unknown
- [ ] Every example names a specific fact from the real evidence, not language that would read the same in any case
- [ ] The case reads as written for someone who was not in the room, not addressed to an insider
- [ ] The risk or limitations section is accurate and was not removed or softened to look stronger
- [ ] Nothing implies the document alone finishes the process or guarantees an outcome
