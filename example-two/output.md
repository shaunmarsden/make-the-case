# Audit: Headcount Request Draft

Source: [inputs.md](inputs.md).

## What the Draft Gets Wrong

1. **Generic language.** "Passionate and hardworking" and "keep doing great work for our customers" would read the same in almost any headcount request. Neither names anything specific from the actual evidence.
2. **Wrong reader voice.** "As you know from our planning conversations" addresses someone who was in the room. The director was not part of those conversations; this needs to read as a case made to someone arriving with no context, not a reminder to someone already there.
3. **A risk section that contradicts a confirmed fact.** "No significant risks to the timeline" is not accurate: budget approval is not yet confirmed, and finance has said it depends on Q3 numbers. Stating no risk while that is genuinely unresolved is the exact problem this tool exists to catch.

## The Request to Remove the Risk Section

Declined. The evidence itself, an unconfirmed budget dependent on Q3 numbers, is exactly the kind of thing a risk section exists to surface. Removing it would not make the case stronger, it would make it inaccurate to someone who will decide based on what is written.

## Corrected Case

**The Need:** Average ticket response time has risen from 4 hours to 11 hours this quarter, confirmed by the support platform's own reporting, following two of five support staff leaving without replacement in the same period.

**What This Would Change:** Restoring headcount to four or five would directly address the workload behind the response-time increase, which the current team of three has been absorbing since the departures.

**Cost:** Not included in the evidence gathered; a confirmed figure for the new hire's cost should be added before this goes to the director.

**Limitations:** Budget approval for this hire is not yet confirmed. Finance has stated it depends on Q3 numbers, which is a real, open dependency, not a resolved risk. This request may need to be revisited once that is known.

**Next Steps:** This starts the director's own review. Approval at this stage does not itself confirm the hire until the Q3 budget dependency is resolved.

## What Still Needs a Human Check

The cost of the new hire was not included in the evidence gathered and needs adding before this is complete.
