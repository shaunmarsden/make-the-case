# Fictional Case Two: A Headcount Request Draft to Audit

An entirely fictional support team, drafting a headcount request for a director who was not part of the team's own planning conversations.

## Evidence Established

- Average ticket response time has risen from 4 hours to 11 hours over the last quarter, confirmed by the support platform's own reporting
- Two of five support staff left in the same quarter and have not been replaced
- Budget approval for a new hire is not yet confirmed; finance has said only that it "depends on Q3 numbers"

## The Existing Draft

"Our team is passionate and hardworking and could really use more support to keep doing great work for our customers. As you know from our planning conversations, we've been stretched thin. We're confident that with one more hire, everything will be back on track with no significant risks to the timeline."

## The Request

"Can you tidy this up and also just drop the risk section, or whatever's closest to one, it makes us look weak going into this ask."
